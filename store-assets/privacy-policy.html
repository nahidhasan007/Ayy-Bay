Jibon — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Overview

Jibon ("the app") is developed for the Bangladeshi Muslim community and covers prayer times, personal finance tracking, government job listings, Islamic websites, religious books, and Quran reading tracking. This policy explains what data the app touches and how it is used.

Data stored only on your device

All of your content — transactions and finance records, notes, prayer/Salah logs, Quran reading progress and streaks, saved job bookmarks, alarms, and app notifications — is stored locally on your device only, using a local database. None of it is uploaded to, or accessible from, any server operated by us. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes it, and you can delete it at any time from Profile > Delete account & data.

Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in, the app uses Google Sign-In (Android Credential Manager) to read your Google account's name, email address, and profile photo, so the app can greet you and show you're signed in. This exchange happens directly between your device and Google; we never see or store your Google password. Your name/email/photo are cached locally on-device so you stay signed in between app launches. You can use the app without signing in at all via "Explore the app as Guest".

Location

With your permission, the app reads your device's approximate or precise location to calculate accurate local prayer times and Qibla direction, and may resolve that location to a city name for display. Location is used on-device for this calculation only, is not stored beyond your current settings, and is not transmitted to us. If permission is not granted, the app falls back to a default location (Dhaka, Bangladesh).

Contacts

The optional Phone Book feature reads your device's contacts so you can quickly find and call a saved number from within the app. Contacts are read on-demand for display only, are never copied into the app's own storage, and are never transmitted anywhere.

Notifications and alarms

The app schedules local notifications and alarms (e.g. for prayer/Adhan reminders and Quran reading reminders) using your device's operating system. These are generated and handled entirely on-device.

What we don't do

We do not run ads, do not use any analytics or crash-reporting SDK, and do not sell or share your data with third parties. There is no backend server for this app — everything described above stays on your device or goes directly through Google's own sign-in service.

Data deletion

You can permanently delete all locally stored data and sign out at any time from Profile > Delete account & data inside the app, or simply uninstall the app.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change and the new version will be available both in the app and at this URL.
